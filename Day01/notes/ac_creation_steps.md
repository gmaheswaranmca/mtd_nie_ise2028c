## AWS Account Creation and Management Console Setup

1. Open Google or Bing and search for AWS Free Tier.
2. Open the official website: [https://aws.amazon.com/free/](https://aws.amazon.com/free/)
3. Click Create an AWS Account.
4. Enter your root user email address.
5. Enter your AWS account name.
6. Click Verify email address and enter the verification code received by email.
7. Create and confirm your root user password.
8. Select the Free plan and continue.
9. Enter your contact information:
   - Full name
   - Country code and mobile number
   - Country or region
   - Address, city, state and postal code
10. Read and accept the AWS customer agreement.
11. Enter your billing information.
12. Select an available payment method, such as UPI AutoPay, if offered.
13. Complete payment method verification.
14. Complete identity verification using an available method.
15. Verify your mobile number using the OTP.
16. Complete any remaining sign-up steps and wait for account activation.
17. Open the [AWS Management Console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/).
18. Select Root user as the sign-in type.
19. Enter your root user email address and password.
20. Complete MFA or any additional verification requested.
21. Open Console Home.
22. Explore the Services menu and Search bar.
23. Check the selected AWS Region and choose your preferred Region, such as Asia Pacific (Hyderabad), if available.
24. Open the account menu and check your account name and account ID.
25. Open Billing and Cost Management and check your available credits, Free plan expiry date and usage.
26. Enable MFA for the root user.
27. Verify that your AWS Management Console is ready for learning and practical exercises.

Important: Use each student's own email address, contact details, payment method and identity information. Never share root credentials.

## Step 12: Select a Payment Method

1. On the AWS sign-up page, open the Billing Information section.
2. Set the billing country to India.
3. Review the billing address and ensure it is correct.
4. Select a payment method offered by AWS, such as UPI AutoPay, if available.
5. Read the payment authorization instructions carefully.
6. If UPI AutoPay is selected, AWS may display a QR code or provide another UPI authorization method.
7. Open the student's UPI app, such as Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm.
8. Scan the QR code or follow the displayed instructions.
9. Review the merchant name, authorization purpose, mandate limit and other details shown in the UPI app.
10. Approve the authorization only if the details are correct and the student understands the terms.
11. Return to the AWS sign-up page and continue.

Important for students: AWS may request a small refundable transaction to verify the payment method. The amount and authorization process can vary. A UPI AutoPay mandate limit is not necessarily the amount AWS will charge immediately; it defines the authorization limit. Students must read the actual mandate details before approving.

## Step 13: Complete Payment Method Verification

1. Wait for the UPI app to confirm the authorization or payment.
2. Return to the AWS sign-up page.
3. Click Verify, Continue, or the equivalent option displayed.
4. Wait for AWS to confirm the payment method.
5. If verification fails, check the UPI app for a pending request or failed transaction before trying again.
6. Do not approve repeated mandates or payment requests without understanding why they are being requested.

### If students want to remove UPI AutoPay after registration

Students may want to remove the mandate after completing AWS registration. Explain the following:

1. First, confirm that the AWS account has been successfully activated.
2. Open the UPI app used during registration.
3. Find the Autopay, Mandates, UPI Mandates, or Recurring Payments section. The name varies by app.
4. Locate the mandate associated with AWS or the payment provider identified during authorization.
5. Open the mandate details and verify that it is the correct one.
6. Select Cancel, Revoke, or Pause, as available. To stop future authorizations, cancellation or revocation is generally the relevant option.
7. Authenticate the cancellation if prompted.
8. Check the mandate status and confirm that it shows Cancelled, Revoked, or an equivalent status.
9. Save the confirmation for reference.

Important warning: Removing UPI AutoPay does not cancel the AWS account, and it does not guarantee that AWS will continue to provide uninterrupted access. AWS may require a valid payment method for billing or account verification. Students should check their AWS billing settings and payment requirements before revoking the mandate.

For a training lab, the safest approach is to use the Free plan, monitor usage, and avoid creating resources that can incur charges. Do not promise students that cancelling AutoPay makes the AWS account completely free of charges.

## Step 14: Complete Identity Verification

1. On the AWS sign-up page, open Confirm your identity.
2. Select the primary purpose of registration, such as Personal Use, if that accurately describes the account.
3. Select the ownership type, such as Individual, if applicable.
4. Choose one of the identity-verification methods offered by AWS.
5. If an Indian identity document or DigiLocker option is offered:
   - Select the available document type.
   - Follow the instructions to provide or retrieve the required details.
   - Review the name and address displayed.
   - Correct any mismatch using the appropriate process.
6. Read the consent statement concerning identity verification by AWS and its verification provider.
7. Provide consent only if you agree to the stated terms.
8. Click Continue and complete any additional verification requested.

Instructions for students:

- Use your own valid identity document and information.
- Ensure that your name and other details are accurate.
- Never use another student's identity document.
- Do not share identity documents, verification codes or screenshots containing personal information with the class.

The available documents and verification workflow may vary by account.

## Step 15: Verify Your Mobile Number Using OTP

1. On the Confirm your identity page, check the country or region code. For India, it is `+91`.
2. Enter your mobile number or confirm the number already displayed.
3. Click Send SMS or the available verification option.
4. Wait for the verification code to arrive on your mobile phone.
5. Enter the OTP on the AWS sign-up page.
6. Click Continue.
7. If the OTP does not arrive, check the mobile number and request another code using the available option.
8. Complete any additional verification requested by AWS.

Instructions for students:

- Use a mobile number that you can access during registration.
- Never share the OTP with another student or trainer.
- Do not share your screen while entering passwords, OTPs or payment authorizations.
- Wait for AWS to finish account setup and confirm activation before starting practical exercises.

### Final checkpoint

- Payment method verification is complete.

- Mobile OTP verification is complete.
- AWS account activation is successful.
- They understand how to check and revoke a UPI AutoPay mandate.
- They understand that revoking AutoPay does not remove existing AWS charges or guarantee continued account access.

If students cancel UPI AutoPay after creating their AWS account, they may need to authorize a payment method again before AWS can process future charges. They should not assume that adding UPI again is always possible directly from the console; the available options depend on the account's billing status and AWS's current payment workflow.

## Steps to set up UPI AutoPay again

### Step 1: Sign in to AWS

1. Open the [AWS Management Console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/).
2. Sign in using the student's root user email address and password.
3. Complete MFA if prompted.

### Step 2: Open Billing and Cost Management

1. Open the account menu in the top navigation bar.
2. Select Billing and Cost Management. Alternatively, search for it in the console search bar.
3. Open Payment preferences or Payment methods, depending on the console interface.

### Step 3: Add UPI AutoPay again

Follow AWS's documented procedure:


AWS Billing



1. Select Payment preferences.
2. Click Add payment method.
3. Choose UPI AutoPay.
4. Enter the student's UPI ID and billing address, or use the existing address if offered.
5. Click Add payment method.
6. Click Verify.
7. Follow the instructions to open the UPI app or scan the displayed QR code.
8. Review the mandate details and approve the authorization in the UPI app.
9. Return to the AWS console.
10. Under Default payment preferences, verify that UPI AutoPay shows AutoPay enabled.

Official instructions: [Managing payments in India — AWS Documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/awsaccountbilling/latest/aboutv2/edit-aispl-payment-method.html).

## Important 

- If a student revoked the mandate only in the UPI app, check the AWS Payment preferences page first. The AWS-side status may need attention.
- If Add payment method → UPI AutoPay is available, follow the steps above.
- If the option is missing or AWS rejects the setup, check the account's registration and billing status or contact AWS Support.
- Students can also use UPI Scan and Pay to pay an outstanding bill manually, if that option is available. This is different from enabling recurring AutoPay.
